# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Existing: static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS (no build), Supabase backend via assets/js/api.js, deployed on GitHub Pages or Vercel.

## Users
Students and people at the college, mostly on their phone, right after discarding something and scanning the QR Code on a bin. The team (Jovem Aprendiz class) uses desktop/projector only for the ranking and for printing QR sheets.

## Product Purpose
Laços Ambientais is a company created by a Jovem Aprendiz class to make the college greener. The site teaches what goes in each CONAMA 275 bin, lets people log discards for a monthly competition (most items, most days), shows the ranking, and redeems a popcorn voucher from a quiz game.

## Positioning
A campus-run recycling campaign tied to physical bins and QR Codes, with a monthly competition and a game, made by the students themselves.

## Operating Context
Pages: index, lixeira (QR target, registers discards), ranking, pipoca (one voucher per name, live clock to defeat screenshots), privacidade (LGPD), qrcodes (team-only printing A4 sheets). Rules: 1–10 items per entry, max 5 entries per person per day, 1 min interval, person identified by phone.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Copy, pages, forms, data flows and Supabase behavior stay intact. On 2026-10-12 the user asked for a more dynamic site ("várias dinâmicas") and allowed layout changes, so the design may add interactive learning and feedback devices built only from existing content and data (the "Onde isso vai?" sorter, the live top 3 on the home page). Bin colors follow CONAMA 275 (blue paper, red plastic, green glass, yellow metal, brown organic) and must stay.

## Brand Commitments
Name "Laços Ambientais", friendly student voice in pt-BR. CONAMA bin colors.

## Evidence on Hand
No logo file, photos or testimonials exist; do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles
- Mobile-first, one-handed, fast right after scanning a QR Code.
- The bin colors are the product's color language.
- Competition should feel rewarding but the rules stay clear and honest.
- Personal data (name, phone) is handled with LGPD care and clear consent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable contrast on every bin color, large touch targets, reduced-motion respected.
