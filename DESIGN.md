# Design System: Laços Ambientais

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "O Cordão"**

A single cord ties the campus to its bins. Five strands in the CONAMA 275 bin colours are braided in SVG into one helix: azul for papel, vermelho for plástico, verde for vidro, amarelo for metal and marrom for orgânico. The cord passes over and under the headline. It loops, unbraids above the bin lanes, and each strand turns off to run along the top of its own bin. When someone logs a discard, their bin's strand closes into a bow. The cord is the system's one recurring object. It serves as the divider, the ranking measure, the string that holds every label and the bow that signs off the footer. Pages are built around where the cord goes.

Everything else stays quiet so the cord can carry the colour. The ground is cool concrete-light and the ink is graphite. There is one display face at one heavy weight. Surfaces are flat, with no shadows, and controls are pills and circles. A bin colour appears only as a strand or as the field that strand floods. Each strand also has its own weave, so a bin can be read by texture before hue: satin for papel, twist for plástico, a hollow double line for vidro, beads for metal and knots for orgânico.

The density is mobile-first and one-handed, because people arrive from a QR Code beside a bin. Type is large, touch targets run from 44 to 56px, and the layout is one column that splits 7/5 from 760px. This world replaces an earlier torn-poster (décollage) look, which is retired. It also refuses the generic eco landing page: no leaf ornaments, no mint gradients, no grids of equal rounded cards.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One five-strand SVG braid in the CONAMA colours, drawn along its length as you arrive and as you scroll.
- Each bin has its own weave, so it never depends on hue alone.
- Cool concrete ground (#eef0ec), graphite ink (#15181b), and bin colour only as strands and the fields they flood.
- Funnel Display 800 for display type, capped at 6rem; Funnel Sans for reading; tabular figures for every count.
- Hang tags on string serve as labels; they swing when pulled.
- Flat surfaces. The only depth is the helix passing behind itself.
- Bands run full-bleed, with the strand entering from beyond the screen edge.

## Colors

The palette is a quiet concrete-and-graphite base, with five saturated bin colours that belong to the cord.

### Primary
- **Graphite Ink** (#15181b): the default `--cor` on every neutral page. It fills the default button, hang tags, ink panels, the competition band, the footer, the ranking strands and the reading-progress strand. On the concrete ground it reads at 15.5:1.

### Secondary
The five strands, which follow CONAMA 275 and must not change. Each one is a strand, the field that strand floods (a lane on hover, its bin's cover, a game choice disc, the success bow), and the colour of the buttons on its bin's page.
- **Papel Blue** (#1f5aa8): papel. Carries folha text (6.55:1).
- **Plástico Red** (#cf3a2e): plástico. Carries folha text (4.70:1). It is also the gincana/pipoca field and the voucher code.
- **Vidro Green** (#23804a): vidro. Carries folha text (4.75:1).
- **Metal Yellow** (#f0b400): metal. Carries tinta text (9.53:1), never folha.
- **Orgânico Brown** (#7b4b27): orgânico. Carries folha text (7.03:1).

### Tertiary
The text-safe versions of the strands (`linha` in `lacos.js`), used when a bin colour becomes text, an icon stroke, a lane icon ring or an empty-knot line on a light surface.
- **Plástico Text Red** (#b8322a): 5.19:1 on the ground. The game also uses it for a wrong answer's border.
- **Vidro Text Green** (#1f7442): 5.04:1 on the ground.
- **Metal Text Ochre** (#8a6500): 4.64:1 on the ground. Yellow never appears as text on a light surface.
- Papel and orgânico use their strand hex as text (5.93:1 and 6.36:1).

### Neutral
- **Campus Concrete** (#eef0ec): the page ground, the `theme-color` on neutral pages, and the `fundo` that the braid's hollow and knotted weaves cut through to.
- **Deep Concrete** (#e1e5df): the track of the ranking tabs.
- **Sheet White** (#fafbf8): cards, the game stage, the voucher, and light text on bin fields and ink.
- **Field White** (#ffffff): input backgrounds, and the hover state of the light button.
- **Concrete Hairline** (#c6ccc5): 1px card borders, ranking rules, the ruler ticks, the voucher's tear line, and input borders at rest.
- **Ink Hairline** (#2c3236): rules between the top-3 rows on the ink competition band.
- **Slate Ink** (#475053): secondary text on the ground (7.21:1) and on sheet (7.96:1).
- **Ash on Ink** (#c9d0cf): secondary text, rank numbers and plain strands on ink (11.38:1).
- **Field Hover Grey** (#9aa39f): input border on hover.
- **Placeholder Grey** (#6b7375): placeholder text in inputs (4.85:1 on white).
- **Strand Shadow** (#0b0d0e): mixed into a strand colour (58% strand, 42% shadow) for any run of the helix that passes behind. It is never used as a fill or a shadow.

### Status
- **Error Blush** (#fbe4e1) with **Error Ink** (#8e1f16): error messages (7.33:1) and the background of a wrong answer in the game.
- **Não-Pode Salmon** (#f08a80): the "Não pode" list on its ink panel: the dashed spine, the ringed markers and the heading icon (7.34:1 on ink).
- **Success Cream** (#fcf1d0): the background of success messages, with tinta text.

### Named Rules
**The Strands-and-Fields Rule.** The five bin colours appear as strands, as the fields those strands flood, and as their own bin's marks on its own surfaces: icon rings, list markers, the knot line and its buttons. They never tint a card, colour a heading or become decoration. Metal Yellow has one recorded exception. On ink it is the signal colour: the focus ring, the one call to action ("Ver o ranking"), the icon in an ink panel's heading and the live dot on the clock. Off ink it is used only for the text-selection highlight and the demo-mode banner. That exception is not a licence for more.

**The Metal Exception Rule.** Every bin field carries Sheet White text except metal, whose yellow field carries Graphite Ink (the `texto` value in `lacos.js`). The swap follows the strand too: on the yellow cover the single strand is drawn in ink with yellow beads. On the success bow, metal's beads are ink.

**The Linha Rule.** A bin colour used as text or as a line on a light surface uses its `linha` value, so every bin stays at least 4.6:1 on the ground.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Funnel Display (with Segoe UI, system-ui, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Funnel Sans (with Segoe UI, system-ui, sans-serif)
**Label/Mono Font:** none. `ui-monospace` appears only inside `code` in the demo banner.

Both families are self-hosted as Latin-subset variable woff2 files (axis 300–800) under the OFL, with `font-display: swap`. Funnel Display is preloaded on every public page, because the hero braid measures the word "laços." after the fonts load (with a 900ms fallback).

**Character:** Funnel Display at 800 is a compact grotesque with tight tracking and some warmth, close in mass to the cord it sits on. Funnel Sans is the same family's calm reading voice, set at a generous 1.6 line-height.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, `clamp(3.5rem, 17vw, 6rem)`, 0.9, -0.04em): page headlines on the ground ("Ranking do mês", "Seus dados, sem mistério").
- **Display Hero** (800, `clamp(3.5rem, 16.5vw, 5rem)` rising to `clamp(4.5rem, 7.4vw, 6rem)` from 760px, 0.9, -0.04em): "Pequenos gestos, grandes laços." Four lines on a phone, two on desktop. It is capped at 10ch between 760 and 1099px and unbounded from 1100px.
- **Display Capa** (800, `clamp(3.75rem, 20vw, 6rem)` rising to `clamp(4.5rem, 8vw, 6rem)`, 0.88): the bin name or page title on a bin-coloured cover. From 760px the pipoca cover uses `clamp(4.5rem, 7vw, 6rem)`.
- **Lane Name** (800, `clamp(2.5rem, 12vw, 4.75rem)`, 0.95, -0.04em): the five bin names in the home lanes.
- **Headline** (800, `clamp(2.1rem, 8.5vw, 3.25rem)`, 0.95, -0.03em): section headings ("Nossas lixeiras", "Onde isso vai?").
- **Headline Panel** (800, `clamp(1.9rem, 7.5vw, 2.5rem)`): headings inside cards, ink panels and the gincana band, with a leading icon at 0.8em.
- **Headline Reading** (800, `clamp(1.6rem, 6.5vw, 2rem)`): headings in the privacy text.
- **Game Item** (800, `clamp(1.35rem, 5.4vw, 1.9rem)`, 1.08, -0.02em): the item printed on the game's hanging tag.
- **Numerals** (800, tabular): counter 5.5rem, voucher code `clamp(3.6rem, 19vw, 5.6rem)`, score 3.75rem, ranking leader 3rem, ranking value 2.1rem. They count up when motion is allowed.
- **Wordmark** (800, `clamp(2.6rem, 13vw, 3.6rem)` rising to `clamp(3.5rem, 6.4vw, 6rem)`, 0.9): "Laços Ambientais" in the footer, on one line from 760px.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.6): all reading text, at most 65ch per paragraph. Privacy text uses a 1.7 line-height.
- **Lead** (400, 1.125rem → 1.25rem, 1.55): the hero intro at 34–36ch. On covers the lead is 600, 1.2rem → 1.4rem, 1.4 line-height, at 30ch.
- **Button** (700, 1.0625rem, 1.15) and **Label** (700, 0.95rem): buttons, form labels and the nav pill.
- **Tag** (700, 0.875rem, 1.25, +0.01em): text on hang tags.
- **Help** (400, 0.9rem, Slate Ink): helper lines under fields and limits ("Máximo de 10 por registro · até 5 registros por dia").

### Named Rules
**The 6rem Ceiling Rule.** No display setting goes above 6rem. Every display clamp, from the hero and covers to the page headlines and the footer wordmark, tops out at exactly 6rem. The large numerals stop below it (5.5rem and 5.6rem).

**The One Weight Rule.** Funnel Display appears only at 800, always with negative tracking (-0.02em to -0.04em) and a line-height between 0.88 and 1.08. Funnel Sans carries everything else, at 400 for reading, 500–600 for emphasis and 700 for labels and buttons. Every number that counts uses tabular figures. Nothing is set in uppercase.

## Layout

The page is a single column with a fluid gutter of `max(20px, calc((100vw - 1200px) / 2))`, so content tops out at 1200px. Bands still bleed edge to edge, by cancelling the gutter with negative margins. Everything sits on the concrete ground, and sections are separated by distance and by the cord, never by boxes.

- **Rhythm.** Bands pad 28px vertically (36px from 760px). Cards stack 18px apart. The hero to the lanes is 44px (96px wide). The lanes to the game is 76px (120px wide). The last section to the footer is 72px, which drops to 0 when the competition band adjoins it, so the ink bands meet with no seam.
- **Tag clearance.** Wherever a hang tag hangs from a strand, the next block starts about 70px below that strand (70px on covers and the voucher, 72px under a page header), leaving room for the string and the swing.
- **Two columns from 760px.** The bin page and the ranking split 7fr/5fr with a 28px gap. On the bin page the registration card is sticky at 16px. Covers and page headers use the grid areas `"nome texto" / "fio fio"`, with the right column at most 24rem, a 56px gap, and the strand running the full width underneath. The competition band splits 1fr/1fr with a 56px gap. The gincana band sets text against an auto-width button.
- **Lanes.** Below 760px a lane is `1fr auto`, at least 108px tall. From 760px it is a four-track row `minmax(0, 22rem) 1fr auto auto`, at least 140px tall. The arrow appears on hover.
- **Centred measures.** The pipoca form and voucher sit in a 620px column and the privacy text in a 780px column, both from 760px.
- **Breakpoints.** 760px is the only layout breakpoint, and the braid engine switches its geometry at the same width. At 1100px the hero headline releases its 10ch cap. Hover styles apply only under `(hover: hover)`.
- **Page transitions.** `@view-transition { navigation: auto }`. A home lane and its bin cover share `view-transition-name: lixeira-{tipo}`, so the lane morphs into the cover. The header keeps `topo` and stays in place.

### Named Rules
**The Full-Bleed Band Rule.** The game stage, the competition panel, the gincana band, the bin cover and the footer each run edge to edge with square corners. Their strands start beyond the edge of the screen (x = −20 to −40px) and cross the whole band. A braid never starts or stops inside the gutter. The 22px radius belongs to contained cards only; an ink panel that becomes a band (`.comp`) drops it to 0.

**The Canal Rule.** On the home page the lanes reserve a corridor on the right (56px, 84px from 760px) for the descending braid. No text or icon enters it.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. There is no `box-shadow`, drop shadow, glow or blur anywhere in the build. Surfaces separate by tone (ground, sheet, ink, bin field) and by 1px hairlines. The only depth is real depth inside the braid: each strand's helix has a front and a back, and every back run is painted in the strand colour mixed with Strand Shadow. Text and the cord are layered so the cord can pass in front of some letters and behind others. The layers, in z-order, are: back strands 0, lanes 1, front strands 2, headline and intro 3, the over-layer that crosses letters 4, and the reading-progress strand fixed at 50.

### Named Rules
**The Helix-Only Depth Rule.** Shadows do not exist. If something needs to feel in front, it has to physically cross over something else, the way the cord does.

## Shapes

The form language is cord and tag: round-capped strands, circles, pills, and the blunt pointed hang tag.

- **Strands** always have round caps and round joins. Static strand rules (section dividers, the knot line) are 6px tall with a 3px radius. Plain ink strands (ranking bars, the reading progress) are 4–5px.
- **Pills** (999px): every button, the nav link, the tab track and its thumb, the clock.
- **Circles** (50%): icon rings with a 2.5–3px stroke (lane icon 54 → 64px, cover icon 66 → 104px), the counter buttons (60px), the game discs (48 → 60px) and the list markers (28px).
- **Cards** have a 22px radius. Fields and messages have 14px, the game stage controls and the game tag 16px, score tiles 18px and the voucher 26px.
- **Hang tag.** A rectangle with only its trailing corners rounded (0 9px 9px 0). Its leading end is cut to a point by `polygon(13px 0, 100% 0, 100% 100%, 13px 100%, 0 50%)`, and a 4.5px eyelet is punched by a mask at 18px from that end. It hangs from a string loop 12px wide with a 2px stroke, 42px tall plus any extra drop (`--alca`), and pivots from the top of the loop.
- **Focus** is a 3px solid outline with a 3px offset and an 8px radius.

### Named Rules
**The Over-Under Rule.** Where the cord meets type, it weaves through the letters and never strikes them through. In the hero it runs just below the baseline of "grandes laços.", so the letters stand on it and the descenders of "g" and "ç" cross over it. Inside "laços." it rises to 0.15em above the baseline, passes over the "l" and the "o" on a clipped over-layer, and slips under the "a", the "ç" and the "s". The tied trefoil knots use the same logic: their upper crossings are painted again on top.

**The Hang-Tag Rule.** A label is a hang tag on a string, hung from a strand: from the hero cord, from a cover's single strand, from the game's braid, or from the voucher loop. A tag hangs below the cord after its heading. It never sits above a heading as a kicker.

## Components

### Braid (trança)
The braid is the signature component and the system's main graphic. `cordao.js` draws it as five SVG strands, each following a helix around a centre line, at phases 2π·i/5 in the order papel, plástico, vidro, metal, orgânico. Each strand is split into runs in front (depth ≥ −0.04) and runs behind. Front runs are painted in the bin hex with the bin's weave on top. Back runs are painted darker, in a group underneath. A mask whose dash offset goes from 1 to 0 draws the braid along its length.
- **Hero (`abertura`)**: strand width 7px wide / 5.2px narrow, helix radius 11 / 8.5, pitch 66 / 46. The braid runs just under "laços." (see the Over-Under Rule) and makes one loop (a prolate cycloid, radius up to 15 / 8.5). The loop drops on desktop and rises beside the word on a phone. The braid then turns down into the canal (radius 48 / 26) and unbraids over 170 / 120px into five parallel strands, spaced width + 3.4 / 2.6px apart. Each strand turns left (radius 30 / 20) to run along the top of its lane, ending in a dot 1.05× the strand width. That strand replaces the lane's 6px top rule. On load the braid draws 1700ms (exponential ease-out) to the end of the hero, then follows the scroll at 90% of the viewport. Each lane strand draws in 0.9s once the cord reaches it.
- **Straight braid (`tranca`)**: the game band, the competition band, page headers, the pipoca cover, and the leader's strand in the ranking. Width 6.5 / 5, radius 10 / 8, pitch 62 / 46, gap width + 3. It draws in 1.6s when 40% of it is visible. `fundo` must be the colour of the band behind it.
- The pipoca cover carries the whole braid, not the plástico strand, because the gincana belongs to no single bin.

### Single strand (fio)
One bin's strand across that bin's cover: 12px wide (8.5px narrow), a gentle sine of 2.2 periods with an amplitude of at most 10px, drawn in Sheet White (in ink on metal). The weave is drawn in the field colour, so the strand reads as the field showing through it. It draws in 1.1s after a 0.15s delay. The cover icon and the "Lixeira {cor}" hang tag hang on it.

### Weaves (trama)
Each bin's weave, as `trama(tipo, w, fundo)` draws it over a strand of width w, with the static CSS fallback used for single straight strands (`[data-trama]` → `--trama-bg`).

| Bin | Strand | Text on field | `linha` | Weave | SVG overlay | CSS fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Papel | #1f5aa8 | #fafbf8 | #1f5aa8 | **liso**, satin sheen | white 50% line at 0.22w | white 34% band at 38–62% |
| Plástico | #cf3a2e | #fafbf8 | #b8322a | **torcido**, twist bands | dark 32% dashes 0.32w / 0.5w | −60° bands, 3px / 2px (strand mixed 66% with black) |
| Vidro | #23804a | #fafbf8 | #1f7442 | **duplo**, hollow centre | `fundo` line at 0.36w | sheet-white centre at 32–68% |
| Metal | #f0b400 | #15181b | #8a6500 | **contas**, beads | dark 45% round dots every 1.15w, 0.46w wide | 1.5px dots every 7px (strand mixed 50% with black) |

The twist, bead and knot weaves walk slowly along the strand (one period every period × 0.14s, linear, about 7px/s), and only while that braid is on screen. Satin and hollow stay still.

### Knots (nós)
These count items on the registration card. There are ten 44px cells on a 6px base strand in the bin's CSS weave. An empty cell is an open overhand loop in the bin's `linha`, 2.2px, with a gap where the loop crosses itself. A counted cell is a trefoil knot: a 4.6px ink outline under a 2.6px stroke in the bin colour, with the upper crossings painted again so the strand passes over. A new knot tightens in 0.45s from 1.7× scale and −35°. Tapping a knot sets the count. On hover an empty cell tints to 35% of the bin colour. Ten is the per-entry cap, so the knots show the limit directly. A counter sits above: ± buttons as 60px rings with a 2px ink stroke, and the number at 5.5rem, which drops in over 0.38s.

### Lanes
The home page's five bin rows. Each lane carries its bin's strand as its top rule, the bin name in Lane Name type, a "Lixeira {cor} · {first item}" line in Slate Ink, and an icon ring in the bin's `linha`. On hover or focus the bin colour floods the full bleed of the row from the top (`clip-path` from `inset(0 0 100% 0)` to `inset(0)`, 0.55s), the text turns to the field's text colour, the icon rotates −12°, and from 760px an arrow slides in 12px. Each lane morphs into its bin cover on navigation.

### Hang tag (etiqueta)
An ink tag (or a Sheet White tag, `.clara`, on bin fields) in Tag type, with padding 9px 18px 9px 34px, cut and punched as described in Shapes. A tag marked `data-balanca` can be dragged sideways. It follows the pointer at 0.4° per px, up to ±38°, and swings back on a damped spring (stiffness 0.055, damping 0.93 per frame). A tag marked `data-balanca="entra"` gets a 3.2 push 650ms after load. On the home page, the tag hangs from the cord just after the loop on desktop and at the foot of the "l" on a phone, and its string lengthens (`--alca`) to reach the raised cord.

### Buttons
- **Shape:** a full pill (999px), at least 56px tall, with padding 14px 26px and a 2px border in the fill colour. Full width below 760px, auto width above it.
- **Default:** the page's `--cor` with `--sobre-cor` text. That means ink on neutral pages, the bin colour on a bin page (ink text on metal), and ink on the pipoca page.
- **Variants:** light (Sheet White with ink, for bin fields and the gincana; ink on the metal cover), yellow (Metal Yellow with ink, the one call to action on the ink competition band), secondary (transparent with an ink outline).
- **Hover / Focus:** on devices that can hover, the fill darkens to 84% toward black over 0.25s. The light button goes to pure white and the secondary button fills with ink. The icon nudges 4px in its direction of travel (the back arrow nudges left). Pressing scales to 0.98. Focus uses the page's focus colour (see Navigation). Disabled is 65% opacity with a `progress` cursor, and the label changes ("Registrando…", "Estourando…").

### Inputs / Fields

### Motion and reduced motion
One easing curve, `cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)`, governs every transition, entrance and strand draw. JavaScript tweens use the matching exponential ease-out, `1 − 2^(−10t)`. Hover colour changes take 0.2–0.3s, icon moves 0.35–0.45s, the lane flood 0.55s, entrances 0.55–0.9s, and strand draws 0.9–1.7s. Page transitions fade the old page out in 0.2s (ease-in) and raise the new one 10px over 0.38s, with shared elements morphing in 0.55s.

### Named Rules
**The Real Strand Rule.** Every strand of the cord is drawn as SVG by `cordao.js` (`abertura`, `tranca`, `fio`, `laco`). A strand is never replaced by CSS stripes or gradients. The CSS weave (`[data-trama]`) is allowed only for one straight, static strand of a single bin, 4–6px tall, in its own weave: the knot line, the section dividers and the printed QR sheets.

**The Own Weave Rule.** Each bin keeps its own weave everywhere it appears: papel liso, plástico torcido, vidro duplo, metal contas, orgânico nós. A weave is never swapped, merged or dropped, and never lent to a surface that is not that bin.

**The Draw-Once Rule.** A strand draws along its own length and never retracts. Scrolling back up leaves the cord where it had reached. Weaves walk only while their braid is on screen.

**The Strand-as-Measure Rule.** When the cord carries data, its length is the data. Ranking strands are cut to true proportion on a shared ruler, the leader holds the whole braid, and there are exactly ten knots because ten is the cap.

**The Still Cord Rule.** Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, everything rests in its final state. Every CSS animation is off. The braid is drawn completely at once, with no intro and no scroll draw. Weaves stand still, tags do not swing or take pushes, numbers show their final value, and smooth scrolling is off. The lane flood becomes a 0.2s opacity fade instead of a wipe. Icon, arrow, disc and tab-thumb transitions are removed. Page transitions shorten to 0.12s. In the game, the next item waits 1400ms instead of 1100ms. Colour remains the only feedback.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** draw every cord, braid, lane strand and bow with the `cordao.js` engine, and pass the colour of the band behind it as `fundo` (#eef0ec on the ground, #fafbf8 on sheet, #15181b on ink, the bin hex on a cover), so the hollow and knot weaves cut through to the right surface.
- **Do** carry each bin's weave wherever that bin appears: papel liso, plástico torcido, vidro duplo, metal contas, orgânico nós.
- **Do** flood a whole field with a bin colour and pair it with Sheet White text, or Graphite Ink on Metal Yellow. Use the `linha` value when the bin colour becomes text or a line on a light surface.
- **Do** cap display type at 6rem and set it only in Funnel Display 800 with negative tracking. Set every count in tabular figures.
- **Do** run bands full-bleed with square corners, and start their strands beyond the edge of the screen.
- **Do** hang labels as tags from a strand, with the 12px string loop, the pointed end and the punched eyelet, and leave about 70px below the strand for the swing.
- **Do** keep touch targets at least 44px, and 56px for buttons and fields.
- **Do** make reduced motion show the finished state at once: the cord fully drawn, the weave still, tags fixed and numbers final.

### Don't:
- **Don't** replace a strand with CSS stripes or gradients. No five-colour diagonal stripe (`--corda` is not a strand) and no striped band standing in for the braid.
- **Don't** give a bin another bin's weave, or put a bin weave on a surface that is not that bin.
- **Don't** use `box-shadow`, drop shadows, glows or blur. Depth is the helix passing behind itself.
- **Don't** use bin colours as decoration: no tinted cards or panels at rest, no coloured headings, no gradients. Light tints of a bin colour (13–35%) appear only as state: the correct game choice, an empty knot on hover, the returning-visitor strip.
- **Don't** bring back the eco landing page (leaf ornaments, mint gradients, grids of equal rounded cards). The leaf drawing belongs only to the orgânico bin icon.
- **Don't** bring back the torn-poster/décollage world: no ripped edges, collage layering or paper grain.
- **Don't** set display type above 6rem, use Funnel Display below 800, set text in uppercase, or use monospace as costume.
- **Don't** put a label or kicker above a heading. Tags hang from the cord, after the heading.
- **Don't** round a full-bleed band, or let a strand start or stop inside the gutter.
- **Don't** strike text through with the cord. It weaves over and under the letters.
- **Don't** let a strand retract on scroll, or animate a weave that is off screen.
