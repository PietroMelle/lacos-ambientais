Scope: whole site (index, lixeira, ranking, pipoca, privacidade, qrcodes). Mode: Persuade (index), Operate-light (lixeira form, pipoca), Read (privacidade).
Audience: college students/staff on phones right after scanning a bin's QR Code. Job: learn what goes in the bin, log a discard, check the monthly ranking, redeem popcorn.
Constraints: page structure, forms, JS behavior and Supabase calls preserved; CONAMA bin colors carry each bin. User wants it professional and dynamic; must not read as generic eco/NGO template, childish/school project, or dark/tech.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One five-strand cord, braided from the CONAMA colors, ties the campus to each bin. It knots into the brand, unbraids into five strands that become the five bins, and pulls into a bow when you log a discard. Refuses the eco landing page (leaf icons, mint gradient, equal rounded cards) and the old torn-poster wall.

OWN-WORLD: Cool concrete-light ground (#eef0ec) and graphite ink; the five bin colors only as strands and the fields they flood. Each strand has its own weave (papel satin, plástico twisted, vidro hollow double, metal beaded, orgânico knotted), so bins read without colour. Funnel Display 800 for display, Funnel Sans for reading and data with tabular figures; hang tags on string for labels; no mono costume, no eyebrows, no shadows-as-decoration.

STORY: Visitor sees "Pequenos gestos, grandes laços." with the cord weaving over and under its letters, follows the strands down into five bin lanes, taps one, learns what goes in it, ties knots to count items, and sees the cord pull into a bow with their score.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile: brand + Ranking pill; four-line Funnel Display headline at ~4rem with the braided cord crossing through "laços." and a hang tag; intro line; the first bin lanes rising at the fold. Desktop: headline left 7/12, the braid sweeping full width and dropping into the lanes below.

FORM: Grounded candidate 7 of 7 (Laço: braided five-strand cord), assigned by the roll and chosen by the user, seed bd5bf1f9. Raises: weave-not-hue state (jackfield), visible capacity of 10 knots / 5 per day (j-card), ranking strands at true proportion on a ruler (oscilloscope), whole-module grid (azulejo). Signature interaction: lane morphs into the bin hero via view transition; the strand draws in on scroll; success pulls the strand into a bow.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
